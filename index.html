Home Screen Layout

Configure your app's home screen layout

Choose your home screen tiles below and arrange them by dragging them around in the preview. Not sure yet? No problem — this is just a starting point, and our team will refine it with you during onboarding and any time you need.

Member Account

Favorites

Shows the next upcoming class from your member's list of favorite classes, instructors and locations. This tile has a minimum width of ⅔.

Member Card

Takes members to a digital membership card that members can use for check-in and identification.

My Account

Provides a link to your Self Service/My Account portal.

Notifications

Lets clients view recent alerts and updates sent from your business. This tile has a minimum width of ⅔.

Social Check-in

Allows the client to check into your facility on Facebook, X, etc.

Booking & Services

Free Trial

Highlights a free trial offer prospects can sign up for directly in the app.

Schedule

Displays your class and appointment schedule so clients can browse available sessions. This tile is always full-width.

My Schedule+

Shows the standard schedule but defaults to the client's favorite classes once they set up their Favorites. This tile has a minimum width of ⅔.

Services

Shows clients a list of phone numbers and links to book extra services like spa treatments, personal training, etc.

Content & Engagement

Announcements

Links to your announcements view and shows a rotating preview of current announcement captions. This tile has a minimum width of ⅔.

Articles

Provides a library of articles, blog posts, or educational content for clients to read.

Feedback

Gives clients a simple way to share comments, suggestions, or concerns with your team.

Tell a Friend

Lets clients invite friends to try your app and business with a quick share link.

Images

Gallery

A rotating photo carousel on the home screen. You'll add your images later — the preview shows three sample slides. This tile is locked to a full-width, double-height size (3 columns wide × 2 rows tall).

Image

Share a single image on your home screen. You'll add your image later — the preview shows a sample. Resizes and moves like any other tile.

Business Info

Change Location

Allows clients to switch which location they're viewing in the app if your business has more than one.

Contact & Hours

Links to your business phone number, email, and hours of operation so clients can easily reach you.

Social Media

Links to your social media profiles so clients can follow and engage with your business online.

Tap each network to turn it on or off. Selected networks show a checkmark; unselected networks appear faded. At least one network must be selected when Social Media is enabled.

Please select at least one social network, or toggle Social Media off.
